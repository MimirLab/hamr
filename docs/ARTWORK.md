# On the name and the artwork

## The shape a story takes

In Cleasby and Vigfusson's *An Icelandic-English Dictionary*, **hamr** covers skin and shape, including mythical changes of form. The entry connects it with *skipta hömum*, changing shape. In chapter 7 of *Ynglinga saga*, Odin takes other forms while his body rests.

hamr borrows that image for an audiobook engine. The interpretation behind the page is a story keeping its identity while taking on a new audible presence. The raven, book and flowing ink make that transformation visible. The headphones are our addition to the folklore.

- [Cleasby–Vigfusson, p. 236, hamr](https://www.germanic-lexicon-project.org/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0236.html)
- [Ynglinga saga, chapter 7, Samuel Laing's translation](https://sacred-texts.com/book/heimskringla-or-the-chronicle-of-the-kings-of-norway/the-ynglinga-saga)

## Original project illustrations

These images were made for hamr using OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool. They combine a woodblock-print treatment, Nordic storybook ornament and a listening raven. They do not depict the performers in the cast.

## Generation prompts

### Storybook header

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original wide hero illustration for the GitHub page of hamr, an audiobook engine named after the Old Norse word for skin, form, and the shape a being takes. Primary request: a delightful, soulful Norse storybook illustration about written stories changing into living voices. Scene: an open, beautifully worn storybook in a cozy midnight scriptorium, a small brass recording microphone beside it. Golden ink rises from the pages as a flowing ribbon, becoming a raven in flight and a wolf's profile before dissolving into gentle sound waves. A second small raven wearing oversized listening headphones perches on the book's edge, alert and slightly mischievous, as though supervising the transformation. The images emerging from the book must feel like one continuous act of shapeshifting, not separate clip art. Style: richly textured hand-cut woodblock print mixed with illuminated Nordic folk-tale illustration, confident elegant shapes, tactile paper grain, intricate but restrained interlaced border, warm human charm, an actual picture worth looking at. Composition: panoramic landscape roughly 2.5:1, clear readable silhouettes at GitHub README width, one cohesive scene with generous visual breathing room. Mood: wonder, a warm light in a cold night, quietly humorous. Colors: midnight blue, weathered parchment, warm copper-gold, muted northern teal. Constraints: no text or lettering, no watermark, no real people or existing fictional characters, no horned helmets, no fake runic inscriptions, no corporate stock-art style, no glossy 3D, no photorealism. Opaque finished background.

### The listener

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original small mascot illustration for the GitHub README of hamr, a local audiobook project inspired by Old Norse shapeshifting. Primary request: a wonderfully characterful raven listener, sitting on a closed well-loved book and wearing slightly oversized comfortable headphones. It tilts its head with the pleased, conspiratorial expression of a bird that has just heard a very good story. One feather lifts as a subtle sound-wave flourish. Keep its posture birdlike rather than humanoid. Style: hand-cut woodblock print blended with Nordic folk-tale book illustration, tactile confident feather shapes, warm and witty, handmade imperfect ink edges, finely chosen details that read beautifully small. Palette: deep midnight blue feathers, copper-gold headphones and book details, parchment and muted teal accents. Composition: one centered complete raven and book with comfortable margins, attractive compact silhouette, designed to display at 200 to 250 pixels wide. Constraints: genuinely transparent background, no lettering or words, no background scene, no watermark, no real person or known fictional character, no fake runes, no glossy 3D or corporate clip art.
