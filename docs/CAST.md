# The cast of hamr

**Choose the company you keep between chapters.**

Forty-five voices, all built into hamr. Some bring a little wonder. Some bring gravitas. Some should probably never have been allowed near a microphone, which is precisely the attraction.

Follow a listening link, find a voice that makes you smile, and imagine what it could do with the next book on your shelf.

[Nature and science](#for-the-curious) · [Storytellers](#for-a-proper-story) · [Questionable decisions](#for-questionable-decisions) · [Absolute chaos](#for-absolute-chaos) · [Legends after dark](#for-legends-after-dark) · [Extraordinary company](#for-extraordinary-company)

## Choose a voice

The whole cast is already in the program. Browse it, hear a sample, and choose your narrator:

```sh
hamr voice cast
hamr voice sample nigel-thornberry --play
hamr voice select nigel-thornberry
```

That becomes the voice for new books. Fancy a different companion for just one story?

```sh
hamr open 'My book.epub' --voice cast:cave-johnson --output ~/Audiobooks
```

The command beneath each voice selects that built-in narrator. Your existing books keep the voices you already chose for them.
