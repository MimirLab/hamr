# The cast of hamr

**Choose the company you keep between chapters.**

Forty-five voices, all built into hamr. Some bring a little wonder. Some bring gravitas. Some should probably never have been allowed near a microphone, which is precisely the attraction.

Every narrator has a little invitation. The listening links below play custom introductions generated by hamr, all reviewed by ear. Find a voice that makes you smile, and imagine what it could do with the next book on your shelf.

[Nature and science](#for-the-curious) · [Storytellers](#for-a-proper-story) · [Questionable decisions](#for-questionable-decisions) · [Absolute chaos](#for-absolute-chaos) · [Legends after dark](#for-legends-after-dark) · [Extraordinary company](#for-extraordinary-company)

## Choose a voice

The whole cast is already in the program. Browse it, hear the same introduction as the gallery, and choose your narrator:

```sh
hamr voice cast
hamr voice sample nigel-thornberry --play
hamr voice select nigel-thornberry
```

That becomes the voice for new books. Fancy a different companion for just one story?

```sh
hamr open 'My book.epub' --voice cast:cave-johnson --output ~/Audiobooks
```

The command beneath each voice selects that built-in narrator. Your existing books keep the voices you already chose for them.

> Here’s the plan. We get through the story, keep an eye on the obvious trouble, and look after the people who can’t look after themselves. I’ll handle the rest.

### The Arbiter

*Keith David*

Long memories, immense stakes, and something worth fighting for. Give a space opera or an ancient vow the room it needs to resonate.

> I have followed certainty into darkness and returned with questions. Walk beside me. We shall hear the old stories again, and decide which truths deserve our loyalty.

### HK-47

*Kristoffer Tabori*

Impeccable diction. Questionable intentions. Mechanised science fiction, dark comedy, and the unnervingly polite explanation of a thoroughly bad idea.

> Observation: organic beings create elaborate philosophies to explain remarkably simple mistakes. I shall identify the mistakes. You may enjoy the philosophy while I calculate a more efficient solution.

### HAL 9000

*Douglas Rain*

Calm, orderly, and very attentive to the mission. A cool presence for spacebound fiction, formal announcements, and the strange intimacy of a machine that speaks softly.

> I want this journey to go well for you. Every detail has been considered, every difficulty anticipated. You can relax now. I’ll take care of what happens next.

### Yes Man

*Dave Foley*

Every idea is wonderful. Every request is a pleasure. Surely nothing ominous could come of such unwavering helpfulness. Cheerful dystopias and very sunny absurdity await.

> Of course your plan can work! I’m delighted to help. If anything goes terribly wrong, I’ll be just as enthusiastic about the next plan. We have so many possibilities!
